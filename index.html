How the SIP 500 works

  1. Market hours: 7:30 PM – 2:00 AM ET. Trading is locked outside those hours.
  2. Every drink is a stock with a ticker, grouped into sectors like the real S&P 500.
  3. When you order a drink, tap +. You buy one share at the current price.
  4. Each purchase pushes that stock up 5% and its sector up 1.5%. Everything else dips 0.2%.
  5. The SIP 500 index starts at 500 and tracks the whole market.
  6. Winner = best return %, not most drinks. Buy early into what the crowd orders next.

Opening prices are rough college-bar prices. Signature stocks: GDNT = TD's Clemson Goodnight mini pitcher, PKL = Tiger Town Tavern's Pickletini, JET = Study Hall's frozen Jet Fuel.
