How it works

Every drink belongs to a market sector. When you log a drink, you buy one share of that sector at its current price.

Each purchase pushes that sector's price up 6% and nudges every other sector down 0.5%. So the crowd's drinking drives the market.

Your return = what your shares are worth now ÷ what you paid, minus 1. Highest return wins. Buying early into a sector others pile into later pays off.

Please drink responsibly. Logging is on the honor system.
