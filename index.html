How the SIP 500 works

  1. Market hours: 7:30 PM – 2:00 AM ET. After hours: 2:00 – 4:00 AM ET. After-hours drinks move prices at half strength. Trading is locked 4 AM – 7:30 PM.
  2. Everyone starts with $100 in SIP Bucks. Winner = highest account value (cash + shares) at the closing bell.
  3. Every drink is a stock with a ticker, grouped into sectors like the real S&P 500.
  4. When you order a drink, tap +. You buy one share, paid from your cash. You pay the midpoint between the price before and after your own order moves it (like real-market slippage). One drink every 10 minutes max.
  5. A friend has to verify it. New drinks show in the Feed until someone else taps Verify. Unverified shares are worth $0 and don't move prices.
  6. Sell anytime from your Portfolio. You get the current price minus a 2% bartender tip. Selling nudges the price down.
  7. Prices follow popularity. Each drink's price depends on its share of what everyone's ordering. Popular drinks rise, and drinks nobody orders sink.
  8. Bull market: the whole market rises a little as the night gets busier, so early buyers are rewarded.
  9. Prices only move when someone orders. Recent orders count most: a drink's influence fades by half after 40 more drinks are ordered.
  10. The SIP 500 index starts at 500. Like the real S&P, it's weighted, so the drinks people own the most move it the most.
  11. Strategy: drink what nobody's ordering yet, then sell once the crowd piles in. 🚩 marks anyone logging more than 4 drinks in an hour.

Opening prices are placeholder bar prices until real menu prices are added. Live prices range from 50% to 250% of opening. Signature stocks: GDNT = TD's Clemson Goodnight mini pitcher, PKL = Tiger Town Tavern's Pickletini, JET = Study Hall's frozen Jet Fuel.
